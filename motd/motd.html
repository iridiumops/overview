Iridium Overview

Simple yet powerful overview pack for everyone, every situation and all play-styles. Designed with multiple window layouts in mind, modular and easily configurable.

Features:
- 13 preconfigured tabs
- 38 overview filters
- 3 bracket filters
- 3 dscan filters
- readable ship labels
- standard color-coding
